ACE AVENUE 0.2 - EARLY TEST BUILD (please read me)
=================================================

Thanks for testing Ace Avenue! It's an ASCII casino roguelike. You arrive on Ace Avenue with
a few hundred dollars. Beat the high rollers of five casinos, win each boss's golden chip, and
take on the Baron at the top of the Pinnacle.


NEW IN 0.2 - "LUCKY STREAK"
---------------------------
- Video poker (Jacks or Better) in every casino. Read the pay table on the glass: a 9/6
  machine pays back far more than a 6/5 one. With Card Sense, press B to hold what the
  book says.
- Baccarat tables at the Starlight Lounge, the Velvet Fox and the Pinnacle.
- Your rival, "Dutch" Calloway, turns up on casino floors and plays you at five-card draw
  for a quarter of your bankroll. Beat him three times for his lucky coin.
- Floors change every visit: a tipsy millionaire who'll cut the deck with you, a pickpocket
  hiding among the gamblers (walk into him first!), and "lucky hour" blackjack tables where
  a blackjack pays 2 to 1 (look for the gold, blinking dealer).
- The Players' Club: everything you bet at the house games earns points. Cash them in at
  any cashier cage for drinks, cash back, lucky charms or a comped suite that clears heat.
- Fixed faint grid lines between squares on high-resolution screens.
- Your 0.1 save carries over.


HOW TO RUN
----------
Zip: unpack it anywhere and run AceAvenue.exe. Portable exe: just run it. Installer: installs
with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. Saves live in %APPDATA%\AceAvenue either way.


THE BASICS
----------
- Move with the arrow keys, the number pad, or Q W E / A D / Z X C. S waits.
- Walk INTO things to use them: tables, slot machines, the bar, people, shop doors.
- I = your sheet and pockets, M = message log, ? = help, Esc = menu, F11 = fullscreen.
- Point the mouse at anything for a description.
- A controller works too.

- High rollers stand by their private tables. Challenge them to blackjack or five-card draw;
  you both put up their stake and play until one of you is broke.
- Beat all three on a floor to open the VIP room and face the boss.
- House games (blackjack, slots, roulette, craps) build your bankroll between duels.
- Winning too much raises HEAT. At 100 heat security walks you out.
- Go broke with nothing to pawn and an unpaid loan, and the run is over.


WHAT TO LOOK FOR
----------------
- Anything that crashes, freezes, or looks wrong.
- High rollers who feel unbeatable or too easy.
- Whether heat feels fair.

Have fun, and don't bet the rent.
